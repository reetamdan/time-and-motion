# 2. PRODUCT PURPOSE

Turn multiple CCTV feeds into useful facility-level crowd intelligence.

The important outputs are:

- live person detection
- camera-local person tracking
- zone occupancy
- floor occupancy
- entry counts
- exit counts
- directional movement
- crowd density/congestion
- queue size
- queue state
- staircase traffic
- camera health
- stale/frozen feed detection
- overlapping-camera duplicate suppression
- temporary cross-camera association where evidence supports it
- two-floor live visualization
- historical analytics
- GPU/system telemetry

The system is NOT intended to establish permanent real-world identities.

Do not implement:

- facial recognition
- facial enrollment
- permanent biometric profiles
- demographic inference
- emotion recognition
- persistent gait biometrics

Temporary multi-camera identifiers are allowed only as session/visit-scoped tracking hypotheses.

The system must support:

```text
UNKNOWN
```

when cross-camera evidence is ambiguous.

Never force an identity association just because two people look similar.

# 8. FULL-SYSTEM PLANNING NUMBERS

These are workload calculations, not benchmark results.

Twenty hypothetical 25 FPS cameras:

```text
20 × 25 = 500 decoded frames/second
```

Detector sampling at 5 FPS/camera:

```text
20 × 5 = 100 detector frames/second
```

Detector sampling at 10 FPS/camera:

```text

```


```text
20 × 2 Mbps
```


Do not use these values as actual camera specifications.

# 32. DEMO STORY

2. Detector identifies the person.
3. Tracker assigns local ID.
4. Entrance line crossing occurs.
5. Building occupancy increases.

10. Person approaches stair transition.
11. Floor 1 track disappears.
12. Candidate appears on Floor 2.
13. Time/topology/appearance are evaluated.
14. Confident association creates temporary continuity.
15. Ambiguous association remains UNKNOWN.
